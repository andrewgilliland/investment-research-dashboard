# Investment Research Dashboard

This context presents model-assisted earnings-call risk research for selected companies and reporting periods.

## Language

**Analysis request**:
A selected company symbol and fiscal quarter submitted for evaluation.
_Avoid_: Query, search

**Risk assessment**:
The complete evaluation returned for one analysis request, including its overall risk and constituent factors.
_Avoid_: Result, report

**Risk factor**:
One scored category that contributes to a risk assessment.
_Avoid_: Signal, metric

**Evidence excerpt**:
A passage from the earnings-call transcript that supports a flagged risk factor.
_Avoid_: Quote, snippet
